## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of contemporary Parisian elegance and opulent Middle Eastern olfactory heritage. Created to mirror the sensory richness of artisanal fragrance compounding, the aesthetic balances monumental architectural geometry with warm, intimate tactile nuance. The intended emotional impact is one of intoxicating luxury, bespoke craftsmanship, and grounded indulgence.

The visual direction unites **Editorial Minimalism** with **Jewel-Facet Structuralism**:
- **Bespoke Proportions**: Vast, measured expanses of soft warm-cashmere negative space counterbalance razor-sharp, diamond-faceted frames and deep noir structural bases.
- **Architectural Motifs**: Octagonal geometry, 45-degree chamfers, and slender metallic hairline borders evoke the iconic emerald-cut crystal flacons and gold-embossed caps of fine perfume bottles.
- **Sensory Materiality**: Interfaces layer satiny cream backdrops with glistening amber undertones, delicate gold-leaf dividers, and deep dark chocolate lacquer surfaces.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from rare oud, crystalline ambers, and bespoke glassmaking:

- **Primary Noir Onyx (`#1A1817`, deeper core `#121110`)**: The anchor tone. Evoking black lacquer, charred wood chips, and bold calligraphy, it provides intense structural contrast against radiant light surfaces.
- **Secondary Warm Amber (`#C59A4E`, supporting `#B38636`)**: A rich, antique metallic accent reminiscent of macerated resin and aged gold foil. Used strictly for focal actions, crests, active indicators, and high-prestige badges.
- **Tertiary Champagne Gold (`#E5C378`)**: A pale, luminous gold utilized for hairline borders, shimmering states, fine dividers, and delicate iconography.
- **Neutral Cashmere Cream (`#FAF7F2`, secondary `#F5EFE6`, border tier `#EFE8DC`)**: A warm, rose-tinted ivory surface foundation that replaces cold optical whites, creating a tactile, parchment-like foundation for imagery and typography.

Apply colors with an 80/15/5 distribution rule: 80% Cashmere Cream canvases, 15% Deep Onyx for typography and stark framing, and 5% targeted Amber & Champagne accents.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic haute-couture editorial flair and contemporary geometric clarity:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: High-contrast serifs with vertical stress, razor-thin hairlines, and opulent terminals. Used for brand declarations, fragrance titles, and major category headings. Display titles should favor generous line heights and slight negative tracking on large scales.
- **Body & Captions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, warm, geometric sans-serif that balances modern legibility with soft humanistic terminals, ensuring fragrance note pyramids and editorial stories remain effortless to read.
- **Labels, Accents & Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans` in uppercase)**: Set with wide tracking (`0.14em` to `0.2em`) to mirror gold-stamped luxury flacon labels and ingredient classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is based on a **Fluid Luxury Grid** with generous vertical pacing, mimicking high-end art catalogues and atelier showcases:

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column responsive layout on desktop, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile screens. Maximum content width is capped at 1440px to preserve typographic measure and visual composure.
- **Whitespace & Breathing Room**: Modules are bounded by deliberate, spacious vertical voids. Margins on desktop remain wide (`4rem`), allowing hero bottles and artistic compositions to command focus without clutter.
- **Rhythm Rules**:
  - Vertical section separation relies on `space-xl` multiplied dynamically (often 4rem to 7rem).
  - Editorial duos (such as imagery aligned with olfactive notes) maintain symmetrical vertical gutters and staggered vertical offsets to create an organic, editorial pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain high-fashion luxury, the interface deliberately rejects synthetic heavy drop shadows, opting instead for **Tonal Tiering**, **Micro-Luminescence**, and **Low-Contrast Metallic Hairlines**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: `#FAF7F2` (Cashmere Cream), providing a soft, natural radiance.
- **Mid-Tier Surfaces (Cards & Drawers)**: `#F5EFE6` or pure translucent ivory (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)`) coupled with a subtle 1px border colored `#EFE8DC` or pale gold `#E5C378` at 40% opacity.
- **Elevated Modals & Flacon Previews**: Elevated layers utilize an ultra-diffused, ambient bronze-tinted occlusion shadow (`0 20px 48px -12px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.08)`).
- **Inverted Surfaces (Onyx Vaults & Night Mode Accents)**: Background `#121110` with hairline borders of `#C59A4E` (30% opacity) creating a gilded jewelry-box presence.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Sharp (`0`)** architectural geometry, drawing inspiration from emerald-cut crystal flacons, diamond facets, and gold bar silhouettes:

- **Borders & Edges**: Standard containers, buttons, input fields, and imagery frames feature precise, sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Faceted Motifs**: Specialized display elements (such as featured fragrance tags, fragrance note chips, and key art containers) may incorporate subtle 45-degree chamfered corners (2px to 6px diamond cuts) using CSS clip-paths.
- **Dividers**: Always rendered as single-pixel hairlines in `#EFE8DC` or degraded metallic gold gradients.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("The Atelier Noir")**: Solid `#1A1817` fill, white `#FAF7F2` uppercase text (`label-md`), 0px border radius, with a subtle 1px border in `#1A1817`. On hover, the background transitions to rich `#C59A4E` with an amber-tinted glow.
- **Secondary ("Gilded Contour")**: Transparent background with a crisp 1px border in `#C59A4E`. Text is `#1A1817` in `label-md`. Hover transitions to a soft `#F5EFE6` wash with border darkening to `#B38636`.
- **Text Link / Minimal**: Understated uppercase text with a hairline underline resting 4px below, transitioning to gold on hover.

### Chips & Olfactive Accords
- Rectangular with sharp 0px borders or diamond-chamfered corners.
- Pale cashmere background (`#F5EFE6`), crisp 1px border (`#EFE8DC`), and tracked micro-typography (`label-sm`) in `#1A1817`.
- Fragrance tier chips (Top, Heart, Base Notes) display a mini gold diamond bullet point (`◆`) preceding the note name.

### Cards (Fragrance & Editorial)
- Framed within delicate 1px `#EFE8DC` borders with an inner padding of `space-md` to `space-lg`.
- Imagery is presented with sharp aspect ratios (3:4 or 4:5 vertical proportions) surrounded by generous cashmere breathing room.
- Bottom metadata includes centered Bodoni Moda typography for the fragrance name, topped by Eau de Parfum concentration in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Understated bottom-border-only or full hairline box in `#EFE8DC` over `#FAF7F2`.
- Focus shifts border color to `#C59A4E` with zero outline rings, accompanied by an animated floating label styled in `label-sm`.
- Caret is styled in rich gold (`#C59A4E`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square components with 0px roundedness and 1px `#C59A4E` borders.
- Active state displays an internal solid gold diamond icon or an inner square inset by 3px.

### Fragrance Note Pyramid & Accord Layering Matrix
- A specialized vertical stack displaying Top, Heart, and Base notes separated by hairline gold gradients.
- Includes an interactive "Fragrance Layering Pair" drawer that demonstrates harmonious scent pairings through split dual-card juxtaposed frames.
