## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital boutique experience rooted in Parisian haute parfumerie, centered on the opulent, tactile world of artisanal gourmand fragrance. The brand persona is decadent, sophisticated, intimate, and connoisseurial—evoking the sensory poetry of roasted tonka, molten amber, whipped orchid cream, and smoked vanilla pods.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Sensual Tactile Luxury** and delicate **Glassmorphic** overlays:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, luminous, and deeply textured, avoiding cold corporate sterility in favor of buttery parchment backdrops, rich espresso depth, and polished metallic warmth.
- **Imagery & Presence:** Generous breathing room, slow-reveal pacing, and delicate linear accents that evoke custom letterpress stationery, gold leaf stamping, and heavy crystal flacons.
- **Audience:** Discerning collectors of niche perfumery, luxury aesthetes, and sensorial connoisseurs seeking an immersive olfactive narrative before purchase.

## Colors

The chromatic direction relies on deep culinary and resinous extraction tones set against luminous, warm dairy-and-silk neutrals.

- **Primary (`#2C1810` - Roasted Tonka / Deep Truffle):** The anchor for high-contrast typography, primary interactive actions, and commanding framing elements. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks with organic cacao warmth.
- **Secondary (`#C88A58` - Warm Amber Caramel):** Used for focal accents, luxury badges, active state indicators, and subtle illuminated borders.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Burnished Honey Gold):** Reserved for ornamental details, foil borders, rating marks, and delicate dividers.
- **Neutral (`#F7F2EB` - Whipped Suede / Cream):** Forms the foundational canvas tone (`#FDFBF7` for upper crystalline cards, `#F7F2EB` for page depth and secondary wells).
- **Shadow Tone:** Tinted with soft velvet plum (`rgba(44, 24, 32, 0.08)` to `rgba(32, 12, 22, 0.16)`) to convey velvety, perfumed depth rather than neutral gray.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary drama of **Playfair Display** with the clear, calibrated proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings:** Set in *Playfair Display* with tight negative tracking on display scales. Italicized accent phrases should be used sparingly for notes, accords, or French descriptors (e.g., *Extrait de Parfum*, *Notes de Tête*).
- **Body & Olfactory Specifications:** Structured in *Plus Jakarta Sans* to maintain crisp legibility across ingredient lists, concentration percentages, and perfumer memoirs.
- **Labels & Micro-Accents:** All label tiers utilize uppercase capitalization with generous letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to emulate luxury bottle labels and embossed packaging cartouches.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open gallery exhibition—spacious, unhurried, and disciplined.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column fluid grid system across desktop screens, reflowing to 6 columns on tablet, and a focused 2-column or 1-column stack on mobile.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Deliberate spatial pauses between narrative tiers. Editorial showcase cards (bottle presentation, perfumer quote) consume full-width or asymmetrical 7/5 column pairings.
- **Safe Canvas Margins:** Desktop margins extend to `4rem` (`margin-lg`) to preserve visual intimacy and prevent content from crowding viewport boundaries.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** Gutter and margin scales step down to `1rem` and `1.25rem` respectively, preserving critical text line-length without compressing sensorial imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous frosted glass, delicate burnished hair lines, and warm plum-shadow ambient drifts rather than harsh, direct dropshadows.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - *Base Surface:* `#F7F2EB` (Warm Whipped Ivory).
  - *Elevated Glass Panels:* Translucent ivory fill (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.72)`) backed by a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
  - *Deep Wells:* `#2C1810` reserved for sensory breakout panels, perfumer notes, and high-impact packaging reveals.
- **Shadow Profile:** Tinted softly with roasted plum and espresso pigments (`rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05)` at base elevation, elevating to `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(44, 20, 26, 0.12)` on floating cards).
- **Artisanal Foil Outlines:** Glass surfaces and interactive luxury cards use ultra-fine 1px borders colored in translucent honey gold (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.35)` to `rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.6)` on hover) to echo cold-pressed gold foil stamping.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness standard (0.25rem / 4px base radius, 0.5rem for standard cards, 0.75rem for featured glass modals). 

- **Architectural Crispness:** Soft corners retain the architectural rigor of beveled glass perfume flacons and hand-cut packaging boxes, avoiding rounded, bubble-like consumer software aesthetics.
- **Badges & Micro-Pills:** Circular or pill shapes are applied selectively only to botanical classification chips, batch tags, or concentration labels (`Extrait`, `Absolu`) to evoke wax seals and apothecary medallions.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Roasted Tonka (`#2C1810`) fill, crisp ivory text, uppercase label typography with letter-spacing. Subtle golden glow on hover (`0 4px 20px rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Translucent ivory glass background with a 1px burnished gold border (`#D4A373`) and tonka text. Transitions to molten caramel background tint on press.
- **Text Action:** Playfair Display italicized link featuring a sliding 1px honey-gold underline.

### Note Pyramid (Top, Heart, Base Cards)
- Three tiered, translucent glass cards (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.8)`) stacked with hairline gold separators.
- Each tier displays the olfactive stage in uppercase tracking (`TÊTE / TOP`, `CŒUR / HEART`, `FOND / BASE`) paired with raw ingredient photography badges and tasting chord chips (e.g., *Caramelized Fig, Bitter Almond, Bourbon Vanilla*).

### Product & Olfactory Imagery Cards
- High-aspect ratio frames (3:4 or 4:5 portrait) showcasing tactile flacons and raw ingredients.
- Framed in delicate 1px borders (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25)`). Hovering triggers a gentle ambient lift accompanied by a gold shimmer gradient shift across the card rim.

### Luxury Badges & Accord Chips
- Compact, circular or softly radiused tags carrying concentration details (`28% Concentration`, `Macerated 6 Months`).
- Neutral ivory ground, hairline amber caramel boundary, micro-typography set in `label-sm`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat ivory fields with bottom hairline or full 1px borders in subdued caramel tone (`rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.3)`).
- Focus states intensify to rich burnished honey (`#C88A58`) with zero synthetic blue rings.

### Checkboxes & Sillage Selectors
- Custom square markers with softened corners and gold foil fill marks when selected. 
- Intensity/sillage scales rendered as graduated amber droplets or fine horizontal metered notches.
